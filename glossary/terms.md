# Canonical Glossary

## Household Operating Architecture
A structural approach to household management that treats the home as an autonomous system rather than a calendar to be manually tracked. Replaces reactive planning with designed architecture.

## Mental Load
The invisible structural carrying of household decisions, scheduling friction, and meal planning. Refers to architectural burden, not emotional weight.

## The Table
The convergence point of home, business, identity, and execution. The space where presence happens once the mental load is offloaded.

## The Table Method™
Human rhythm framework: Pause, Clarity, Alignment, Trust. Replaces hustle and survival-mode carrying with intentional rhythm.

## Flo
The Household Operating System. Autonomous execution layer that manages family details, schedules, and meals, removing the mental load from the founder's head.

## Your Morning Anchor
Daily structural reset feature within Flo.

## Flo's got this
Canonical confirmation that the system is carrying the load.

These terms are locked. Never paraphrase, substitute, or soften. All expression agents must use exact canonical language.
