# Framework: Flo — The Household Operating System

**Definition:**  
An autonomous execution layer that manages family details, schedules, and meals, removing the invisible mental load from the founder's head.

**Components:**
- **Your Morning Anchor:** Daily structural reset
- **Context-Aware Meal Planning:** Suggests fast recipes based on daily activity load
- **Grocery List Automation:** Removes decision fatigue from household provisioning
- **Dashboard Visibility:** "What Flo's Tackling First"

**Function:**  
Operationalizes the Table Method by carrying the architectural load the founder was previously holding manually.

**Application Context:**  
Deployed as the execution engine after the founder pauses and defines household architecture.

**What It Replaces:**  
Fragmented apps, sticky notes, mental tracking, and calendar micromanagement.
