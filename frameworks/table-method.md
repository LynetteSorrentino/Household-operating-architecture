# Framework: The Table Method™

**Definition:**  
A human rhythm framework for pausing, reconnecting, and returning to clarity and connection across personal and professional spaces.

**Components:**
1. **Pause:** Interrupting automatic survival behaviors
2. **Clarity:** Identifying what actually matters at the table
3. **Alignment:** Structuring environment and expectations to match capacity
4. **Trust:** Releasing manual control once the architecture is in place

**Function:**  
Replaces hustle and survival-mode carrying with intentional rhythm.

**Application Context:**  
Used when mental load is peaking, presence is fracturing, or the household rhythm is collapsing into reaction.

**What It Replaces:**  
The belief that pushing harder or managing more will restore peace.
